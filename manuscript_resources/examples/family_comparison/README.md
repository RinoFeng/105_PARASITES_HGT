# Fixed 48-species quantity comparison

Run `python examples/family_comparison/run.py` from `manuscript_resources/`.
The input is a frozen subset of `S2/Q_species_counts.tsv`, scenario `augmented_same_reference`,
retaining species marked `entered_function_comparison=True`.

| Column | Definition |
|---|---|
| C | Candidate HGT gene families in one species |
| B | Eligible background gene families in that species |
| N | Candidate HGT gene loci in that species |
| M | Background gene loci in that species |

The fixed clades contain 3 Amoebozoa, 28 Apicomplexa, 11 Euglenozoa and 6 nematodes.
The source label `Metazoa` refers to these 6 nematodes in this example, not all metazoans.

For each of 1,000 draws, three species are sampled without replacement from each clade.
Counts are summed within the selected species. The normalized metrics are C/B and (N/C)/(M/B).
Each metric is divided by its counterpart for the sampled nematodes. The geometric mean and central
95% range of these ratios are reported. Seed: 20260922; NumPy Generator/PCG64.
The unadjusted metrics use C/3 and N/C. Family occurrence is counted separately in each species;
this is not the union of distinct family IDs across the entire study.

The central range describes variation among selected species subsets. It is not a confidence interval
from independent evolutionary replicates and does not supply a formal P value.
All 9 normalized component-by-clade contrasts are checked against the saved analysis table,
including total locus ratios. Plots show the family and within-family components.

`expected/normalized_contrasts.tsv` is the pre-existing expected result, not generated by the example.
`expected/validation_preparation.json` records the initial successful check.
The script does not add loci or change HGT assignments.
